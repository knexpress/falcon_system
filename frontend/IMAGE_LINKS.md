# Falcon Revive — high-quality image links

All images are free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).

## How to download in Chrome
1. Open the **Unsplash page** link.
2. Click **Download free** (top right).
3. Save into `frontend/public/images/` if you want offline copies.
4. Or keep using the **direct URL** (already wired in the site).
